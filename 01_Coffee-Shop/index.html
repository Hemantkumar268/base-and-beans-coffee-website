Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We've Got Answers

What are your store operational hours?

We are open Monday to Sunday from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM at all our main cafe locations.

Do you offer dairy-free or vegan milk alternatives?

Yes! We offer Almond, Oat, and Soy milk alternatives for all our espresso and specialty drinks.

How fresh are your packaged coffee beans?

Our beans are small-batch roasted daily and packed in degas valve bags to guarantee peak freshness.

Is your packaging eco-friendly?

100%! All our takeaway cups, lids, and bean packaging are 100% biodegradable and compostable.
